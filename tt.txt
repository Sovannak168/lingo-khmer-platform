# PROJECT: Professional Chinese & English Learning and Examination Platform

## 1. Your Role

Act as a Senior Full-Stack Developer, UI/UX Designer, Database Architect, AI Engineer, and Educational Technology Specialist.

Your task is to design and develop a professional, production-ready online learning platform for students who want to learn Chinese and English and prepare for internationally recognized language proficiency examinations.

The platform must be scalable, secure, responsive, modern, and easy to use.

## 2. Project Objectives

Build a bilingual language-learning platform supporting:

**Chinese Language**
- HSK Level 1–6 preparation.
- HSK vocabulary and grammar.
- Chinese pronunciation (Pinyin).
- Chinese characters (汉字).
- Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking.
- Mock HSK examinations.

**English Language**
- IELTS Academic and General Training preparation.
- TOEFL iBT preparation.
- TOEIC preparation.
- Cambridge English exam preparation.
- English vocabulary and grammar.
- Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking.
- Mock examinations.

The platform must help students measure their proficiency and track their learning progress.

Important: This platform provides exam preparation and estimated practice scores. It must not claim to issue official HSK, IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC, or Cambridge certificates.

- IELTS Academic.
- IELTS General Training.
- TOEFL iBT.
- TOEIC.

- Writing
- Speaking

Question types:
- Multiple Choice.
- True / False.
- Fill in the Blanks.
- Matching.

- Essay Writing.
- Audio-based Questions.
